How to Calculate Real Amazon FBA Profit Per Product (2026)

AnnMax · November 17, 20268 min read

Why the Sticker Price Is Not Your FBA Profit

A product that sells for €29.90 does not put €29.90 in your account. Amazon takes a referral fee, FBA takes a fulfilment fee, storage accrues, VAT has to be accounted for, and that is before you count what you actually paid for the unit. Sellers who only look at the price regularly discover — too late — that a ‘best seller’ was losing money.

If you want to calculate real Amazon FBA profit per product, you need all of those numbers at the moment you are looking at the listing, not in a spreadsheet you update on Sunday. MarginMate does the calculation right on the Amazon page.

How MarginMate Calculates Real Amazon FBA Profit Locally

MarginMate reads the product page you are already on and computes the margin locally in the extension. It does not send the ASIN, the price, or your cost inputs anywhere. There is no pricing server and no account behind the calculator.

That is the core of how to calculate real Amazon FBA profit: take the live numbers from the page, combine them with your own cost inputs, and do the arithmetic where the data already is. The result appears as a breakdown, not a single optimistic number.

Every Fee, Line by Line

The output is deliberately itemised. Referral fee, FBA fulfilment fee, storage, VAT, your landed cost, and any other cost you entered each get a line. You can see which fee moved when Amazon changed its schedule, instead of guessing why a margin dropped.

An itemised breakdown also makes the tool honest about uncertainty: if a fee estimate is based on size tier or weight, the line says so. A single profit figure hides assumptions; a list of lines exposes them.

Sixteen Marketplaces and a Fee Table That Refreshes Itself

The calculator covers 16 Amazon marketplaces. Fee schedules change several times a year, so MarginMate refreshes its rate table daily. When the refresh fails — a changed endpoint, a network hiccup, a blocked request — it silently falls back to the built-in table instead of breaking the calculator or throwing an error at you.

That fallback matters more than it sounds. A fee tool that goes blank the moment a source moves is worse than one that keeps working with slightly older numbers and marks them as such. The daily refresh is an optimisation; the built-in table is the guarantee.

Your Costs and Saved Products Stay Local

Your cost inputs and the products you save are stored in chrome.storage.local. They stay in your browser profile, on your machine. There is no cloud copy of your margins, no shared workspace, and no server that learns which products you are researching.

For a seller, that is not a small detail. Product research is commercially sensitive. Keeping the numbers local means the tool can help you decide without becoming another place your business data accumulates.

MarginMate is free to use, and Pro adds more capabilities. We would rather describe it that way than invent a feature list: the free calculator already covers the full per-product breakdown and the 16 marketplaces, and Pro extends it further.

MarginMate is available on Edge and Firefox now; the Chrome version is coming soon. On any browser, the promise is the same: calculate real Amazon FBA profit per product, locally, with the fees shown line by line — a number you can trust because you can see how it was built.

MarginMate がローカルで Amazon FBA 利益を計算する方法

これが Amazon FBA 利益を計算する核心です。ページのリアルタイム数値を取り、自分の原価入力と組み合わせ、データがすでにある場所で計算する。結果は楽観的な一つの数字ではなく、内訳として表示されます。
